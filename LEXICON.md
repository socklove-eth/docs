# SockLove Docs Lexicon

> Check a word here before inventing one.

---

In the beginning was a sock, and then a word for it. These are the words of
SockLove: the world they make, who walks in it, the chain it stands on, the Rite
it keeps, the places it builds, what it has promised, the pipes beneath, the
words it borrowed, and the one it keeps for a smile.
