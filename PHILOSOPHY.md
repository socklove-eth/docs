# SockLove Docs Philosophy

> A vow kept by its shape.

---

The foundation under everything
🧦💜 **[SockLove](https://github.com/socklove-eth)** builds. It does not move
when a version number does.

---

## 🌱 Why this exists

SockLove began with one person, and is built for two.

Desire runs on hints, deniability and rules nobody admits to learning. SockLove
makes the implicit explicit, writes it down, and builds it so it cannot bend. It
makes room for heat, for love and filth in one breath, and for every
contradiction a person carries — without shame.

It asks for one thing: **a true yes** — true the way mathematics is true.

Forever — if both want it. The "if" is the whole point.

---

## 🧱 Consent as architecture

A promise is kept by whoever made it. Architecture is kept by its shape.

SockLove builds consent as a shape: two keys, one lock, a contract that opens
for both or for nobody. A yes is never assumed and never inherited: every act
asks again, and the chain remembers who answered. What a vow asks of people,
SockLove asks of mathematics, which does not tire, forget or give in.

---

## 💬 Eight words

They guide every feature, plan and sentence; one that pulls against them has to
earn its place.

1. **Decentralisation.** No landlord, no single point of failure — or of
*betrayal*. Trust mathematics, not management.
2. **Immutability.** What is sealed stays as it was sealed; what must stay
alive changes only by the
💍 **[Ring](https://etherscan.io/address/socklove.eth)**'s hand. Erasure is how
shame talks.
3. **Permanence.** Love that intends to last belongs on something that intends
to last.
4. **Security.** Every vow has enemies. What can be checked is checked, never
trusted, and nothing signs unseen.
5. **Privacy.** Desire is nobody's data. What is never collected can never leak;
what is shown is shown by choice.
6. **Minimalism.** What is not there cannot rot, be exploited, or be switched
off.
7. **Openness.** What SockLove makes is given away, free and public. **DRM is
theatre:** a lock on a picture stops only the person who paid.
8. **Intimacy.** Not everything is content. One door stays shut — not to hide
the beauty, but because what happens behind it is presence, not pixels.

---

## 🧭 The shape of it

Watching will be free. Belonging will cost a vow.

A 🐾 **Stray** will wander in from the dark and watch: every 💎 **Relic**, the
📜 **Myth**, the 🗣️ **Nave**, open to anyone, owed nothing, asked nothing. A
👁️ **Witness** will hold a Relic, and with it the moment it keeps; only a
Witness will pass the one shut door, into the 🚪 **Chamber**. **Nobody will pay
for the beauty. They will pay, if at all, for having been there.**

---

## 🔒 What cannot be bent

Not a paywall with poetry on it. Not erasable, pausable or upgradeable — by
anyone. Not ashamed. Not for speculation.

A Ring at 1-of-1 can still be moved by one hand. That is why the
🧎 **[Proposal](https://github.com/socklove-eth/.github/blob/v1.0/RELEASE.md)**
comes first, and why nothing meant to last is built before the Ring is whole,
save what asks for it. After it, no act that matters is one person's decision;
either may speak, and neither ever for the other. **A vow that could be broken
is a vow. A vow that cannot be broken is architecture.**

---

Two signatures. Both or nothing. Forever.
